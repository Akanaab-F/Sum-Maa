Volunteer With Us

Bring your hands, heart, and skills.

Volunteers are the heartbeat of SUM-MAA Foundation. From packing food items to reading with children or supporting youth empowerment sessions, there’s a place for you.

What volunteers do

  • Help pack and distribute food items.
  • Support school outreaches and empowerment sessions.
  • Assist with logistics during events.
  • Capture stories, photos, and testimonies.

Volunteer commitments

No amount of time is too small. You can volunteer occasionally, monthly, or for special campaigns.

Partnership & Sponsorship

Multiply your impact through partnership.

When organizations, businesses, faith communities, and individuals partner with SUM-MAA Foundation, we are able to reach farther and go deeper in the lives we serve.

  • Partner on specific projects like school outreaches or food drives.
  • Provide in-kind donations (food items, school supplies, hygiene products).
  • Offer professional skills—training, mentorship, media, logistics, or consulting.

Corporate Sponsorship

Align your brand with a trusted, impact-driven nonprofit. Sponsor an outreach, event, or multi-month project and share the story of hope with your customers and staff.

Email: info@sum-maafoundation.org

Church & Community Groups

Invite us to speak, collaborate on outreach, or co-host empathetic service days that bring your community closer together.

Let’s dream and plan together.
