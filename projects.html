Our Projects & Programs

Every project is a story of hope.

From classrooms to street corners, SUM-MAA Foundation projects are designed to feed, empower, and uplift people in tangible and sustainable ways.

Project Categories

Education Support Food Relief Orphanage Outreach Street Feeding Youth Empowerment Skill Development
Education Support Accra • 2025

Back-to-School Hope Drive

We provided books, writing materials, uniforms, school bags, and shoes to children whose families could not afford basic school essentials.

Impact: 200+ students equipped for a confident start to the school year.

Food Relief Community Centers • 2024

Community Food Relief Outreach

Families facing food insecurity received nutritious food packs and hot meals, easing daily burdens and restoring a sense of dignity.

Impact: 150+ households supported with food assistance.

Orphanage Outreach Orphanages • 2023–2025

Hearts & Hands Orphanage Visits

We visited orphanages with clothing, sanitary items, food, and gifts—creating safe, joyful spaces for children to feel loved and seen.

Impact: 300+ children reached with practical care and emotional support.

Street Feeding City Streets • 2022–2025

Meals of Mercy Street Feeding

On busy sidewalks and quiet corners, we served hot meals, water, and words of encouragement to those living on the margins.

Impact: 500+ hot meals served to the less privileged, including disabled individuals and street dwellers.

Youth Empowerment Schools • 2023–2026

Youth Empowerment & Mentorship Sessions

Through motivational talks, mentorship, and skill-building workshops, we inspire young people to dream bigger and believe in their capacity.

Impact: 10+ schools reached, 400+ students engaged in empowerment sessions.

Skill Development Communities • 2024

Skills for a Brighter Tomorrow

We connect youth and adults to basic skills training, entrepreneurship guidance, and hands-on learning that can unlock new sources of income.

Impact: 80+ participants introduced to sustainable skills and opportunities.
