About SUM-MAA Foundation

Serving with compassion, dignity, and hope.

SUM-MAA Foundation exists to stand beside the vulnerable—with food, education support, and empowerment that restores dignity and opens doors to a better tomorrow.

Our Background

Putting smiles on faces since 2021

Over the years, SUM-MAA Foundation has transformed lives and brought smiles to both children and adults. What began as a simple desire to respond to visible need has grown into a movement of compassion, service, and community.

We go beyond handouts—we build relationships, listen to stories, and walk alongside families, students, and communities as they discover new possibilities for their future.

How we show up

  • Visiting orphanages with clothing and sanitary items.
  • Providing books, writing materials, uniforms, school bags, and shoes.
  • Supplying food and hot meals to school children.
  • Feeding the less privileged and disabled on the streets.
  • Organizing motivational and empowerment sessions for students.

What makes us different

We are intentionally relational, community-rooted, and impact-focused. Every initiative is designed to meet real needs with practical support and heartfelt care.

Vision

Our vision for the world

“Inspiring change, transforming lives, and healing the world through compassion — one step at a time. We are stronger together. The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others.”

Mission

What guides our daily work

“Feeding hopes, healing lives, educating futures, and promoting skill development.”

Every outreach, every meal, every school kit, every empowerment session is an expression of this mission in action.

Core Values

The heart behind our work

These values define how we serve, how we partner, and how we show up in every community we are privileged to walk with.

Compassion

We choose to see, feel, and respond with love to the pain and potential in every person.

Integrity

We handle resources, relationships, and stories with honesty, transparency, and accountability.

Service

We lead by serving, not by being served—placing the needs of others at the center of what we do.

Community

We believe lasting change happens when communities are involved, empowered, and united.

Empowerment

We don’t just give—we equip, mentor, and encourage people to step into their God-given potential.

Impact

We measure success in transformed lives, restored dignity, and hope that keeps multiplying.
