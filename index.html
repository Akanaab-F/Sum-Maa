About SUM-MAA Foundation

Putting smiles on faces since 2021

Founded in 2021, SUM-MAA Foundation has been dedicated to putting smiles on the faces of children and adults through outreach, education, feeding programs, and empowerment initiatives.

Learn more about us

What drives us

We believe every person deserves dignity, opportunity, and hope—regardless of where they were born or what they have.

How we serve

From school support to street feeding and empowerment sessions, we meet people where they are, with compassion and respect.

Our Impact (So Far)
